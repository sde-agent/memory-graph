# Core Intent & Vision: The Photographic Memory Graph

## 1. The Core Purpose: Evoking Feeling, Not Managing Assets
This is **not an asset manager, photo browser, or generic search tool**. 

Its sole mission is to be an emotionally intelligent companion for a lifetime of personal photographs. It exists to do two things deeply:

1. **Daily Evocative Dispatches:** Deliver 1–3 photographs each day that trigger a genuine, visceral memory. Not arbitrary "On This Day" dates, and never superficial matches based on keywords or tags. It must surface moments that make you stop, pause, and *feel* what was happening when the shutter clicked.
2. **Longitudinal Style & Emotional Self-Reflection:** Provide thoughtful, perceptive answers to introspective questions about your creative journey, such as:
   > *"What was my photography style in the 2010s?"*
   > 
   > *"Your primary style was capturing quiet intimacy and protective tenderness, and you still return to those photos today because they evoke that feeling. During that decade, you leaned heavily on a 50mm lens shot wide open at eye-level—not to document events from afar, but to freeze subtle, unposed domestic presence before life changed."*

---

## 2. The Guiding Philosophy: Technical Choices Reveal Emotional Intent
In photography, technical parameters are not dry settings—they are the photographer's physical and psychological posture toward the subject:

* Choosing an **85mm telephoto at f/1.8** to isolate a face while standing two paces back is an act of **tender, empathetic observation**.
* Choosing a **14mm ultra-wide low to the earth** beneath an expanse of rock or coast is an expression of **visceral awe and personal insignificance**.
* Choosing a **50mm lens at natural human eye-height** is a choice to **preserve an authentic memory** as the human eye naturally experienced it.
* Choosing a **long exposure on moving water** or leaving vast empty sky in the frame is an intentional pursuit of **stillness, solitude, and calm**.

By decoding the optics (focal length, depth of field, camera angle, shutter speed, horizon geometry), the system understands the **emotional intent** behind the photograph without guessing.

## 4. What Must Be Filtered Out (The Clutter Intent)
To ensure the memory graph only contains authentic photographic art and lived moments:

* **Strictly Exclude Utility Images:** Zero tolerance for screenshots, receipts, whiteboards, presentation slides, scanned paper documents, or UI mockups. The engine should detect and drop these instantaneously.
* **No Burst Spam:** Avoid bloating the graph with multiple consecutive frames of the exact same moment. Collapse bursts into a single **Canonical Keyframe** (the sharpest, best-focused frame with eyes open), while grouping the rest into the background.
* **De-prioritize Posed Smiles:** The daily memory dispatcher must consciously down-weight formal, posed group photos where everyone stares rigidly at the camera with forced smiles. It must intentionally elevate unposed candid interactions, natural eye gaze, and quiet landscapes.

---

## 5. Landscape & Nature Integration
Nature photography evokes the same matrix quadrants as portraits, but through different physical cues:

* **Quadrant 1 (Nostalgic):** Warm golden-hour light (<3500K), familiar walking trails, dappled sunlight through canopies.
* **Quadrant 2 (Visceral):** Low horizon line (<30%) amplifying towering storm clouds or jagged peaks, fast-frozen crashing surf.
* **Quadrant 3 (Empathetic):** "Intimate landscapes"—isolating a single dewdrop, a solitary leaf, or fine moss texture rather than sweeping vistas.
* **Quadrant 4 (Solitary):** Silky, motion-smoothed water from long exposures, mist or fog isolating a lone tree, vast negative sky.
* **Quadrant 5 (Provocative):** Jagged chiaroscuro shadow contrasts, storm horizons with deliberate diagonal tension.

## 6. The Long-Term Interaction Model
When interacting with this system, the user is speaking with an **expert visual psychologist and master photographic critique**:

1. **The System Understands Life Eras:** It groups photos by meaningful life epochs (decades, child developmental stages, major travels, home relocations) to track how photographic voice evolved alongside life changes.
2. **The System Explains the "Why":** When presenting a photo, it does not just say *"Here is a photo from 2014"*. It articulates:
   > *"12 years ago. Shot at 50mm, f/1.8. Notice how you lowered the camera to child eye-level and let the background dissolve into soft light—preserving a quiet, unscripted moment of focus rather than an artificial pose."*
